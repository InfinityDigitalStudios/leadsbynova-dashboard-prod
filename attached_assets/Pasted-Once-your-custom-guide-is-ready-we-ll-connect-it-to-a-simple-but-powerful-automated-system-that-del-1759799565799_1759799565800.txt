Once your custom guide is ready, we’ll connect it to a simple but powerful automated system that delivers it to your audience and starts collecting valuable insights about their interests.

Here’s how it works:

1. Sharing Your Link
You’ll receive a custom link that can be shared anywhere — in an online ad, a social post, or even a direct message.
When someone clicks that link, they’re taken to your Submission Form Page, where they enter their basic information to access your free guide.

If you’d like to run ads, we’ll also help you create and deploy your campaign through Meta or Google Ads, ensuring your guide reaches the right audience and performs effectively.

To keep everything fully compliant, we’ve incorporated legally required opt-in language for SMS and email into the fine print of your submission form. Each time someone agrees to receive messages, their consent record is automatically stored in your dashboard for safekeeping — giving you clear documentation if it’s ever needed.

2. The Simulated Chat Experience
After clicking to access the guide, they’re redirected to a short, web-based simulated chat — designed to look and feel like a real conversation, but fully automated.
Rather than typing responses, users simply select from a list of multiple-choice answers that help you learn more about their goals, interests, or needs related to your business.

Participation in the chat is optional, as required by law.
If someone chooses not to participate, the system still captures their name, email, and phone number (with the phone field being optional).
If they do participate, however, the chat collects additional, business-relevant details — and at the end, it automatically prompts them to schedule an appointment.

When a user schedules, the appointment details and all their responses are instantly sent to your dashboard and to your email — giving you a complete, ready-to-act profile of your new lead.

3. Delivering the Guide
Once they finish the chat (or skip it), the system immediately sends your guide straight to their inbox — giving them instant access while ensuring you have a verified, trackable lead in your dashboard.

The result is a professional, compliant, and fully automated system that not only attracts attention but also starts building relationships — capturing interest, gathering insights, and setting appointments without lifting a finger.
